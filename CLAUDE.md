# Claude agent entrypoint

Read and follow [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) **in full before any review, implementation, or test**. It defines two separate tracks: **backend engineering may proceed on existing PDP product intent despite incomplete PDP formalization or gtool admission**, while **formal DISCOVERY → BUILD → DELIVER → ENHANCE stage advancement, PDP acceptance/closure, and release** retain their genuine source-current, authorization, proof, and readiness requirements.

Do not turn an unavailable formal gate or incomplete unrelated PDP surface into a blanket ban on coding, testing, refactoring, or logically validating backend packages. Do not treat the engineering permission as an approval to run a second canonical runtime writer, change applied migrations, activate providers, bypass security, fabricate evidence, or release. Follow more-specific nested guidance only where consistent with this separation and the current canonical implementation/source owners.
