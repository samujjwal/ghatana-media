## 1. Accepted foundation and authority

1. **Four independent authored phases:** PDP-0 Product Truth; PDP-1 canonical Domain/Data/Behavior; PDP-2 design language and interface contracts; PDP-3 complete product experience. Each phase must be meaningful and self-contained without reading a historical migration plan. Explorer is a projection outside those phases. A phase's definition/semantic acceptance is not conditioned on production deployment, hardware/provider qualification, or a production Web host unless that exact phase requirement calls for live behavior; these are downstream implementation/release gates.
2. **Single Media owner:** `.product-experience` in `ghatana-media` is the one editable authority for Media product meaning; historical `ghatana/services/media` contracts are migration/implementation observations and compatibility sources, never competing Product Truth.
3. **Shared/Tools/Lifecycle ownership:** Shared owns generic identity, audit, tokens, UI primitives, workflow/messaging, governance, and tracing contracts; Tools owns Product Definition, Experience, design tooling, generic Explorer mechanics, schema verification; Lifecycle owns evidence admission, currentness, closure receipts, and convergence. Media may only consume public owner package/API contracts. No sibling private source imports or Media-specific parallel framework.
4. **Document Intelligence:** MDI-001 stands. Generic OCR, document parsing/extraction and their providers remain outside Media; Media owns temporal scene-text, subtitle, caption and their source/time mapping semantics. The AI Inference owner owns generic model governance/routing, not Media's audio/video/timeline domain models.
5. **Presentation:** `@audio-video/ui#MediaProductRenderer` is the preferred Media-owned React renderer for currently implemented screen families. Both Tools-hosted review and a future production client must mount *that same public export*, with exact fixtures/typed state, action, rendering and error ports. Mere render-command return values and alternate review DOM are not parity. API-only, CLI-only, SDK, event and agent experiences are inspectable without inventing GUI screens.
6. **Licensing and capability availability:** accept the prior Ghatana-first / permissive-third-party *selection policy* and the candidate shortlist, **not** installation, binary/model/codec admission, universal format support, or proof of legal rights. Engines, models/weights, assets, fonts, native packages, patent exposure, distribution profiles and transitive dependency graphs are independently admitted. No permissive-only promise of generic MP4/H.264/AAC transcoding without a qualified provider. Unavailable output formats are explicitly unsupported or routed to an externally qualified/licensed adapter.
7. **No self-approval of independent gates:** a delegated Media owner may decide product intent and mappings; cannot substitute for a truly independent visual or assistive-technology assessment, a third-party owner accepting its public API, a legal license assessment, a measured model/codec qualification, or Lifecycle-generated evidence.

## 2. PDP-0: approved capability and normative-source decisions

**P0-D01 — Coverage is applicability-based, not GUI-based.** Every leaf capability must bind to one or more purpose-specific outcomes and interfaces. Permitted explicit dispositions: `JOURNEY_STEP` (human task); `MACHINE_OPERATION` (API/CLI/SDK/event/agent); `PLATFORM_DEPENDENCY` (named external owner/contract); `SUPPORTING_READ_MODEL` (read-only or observational); `EXCLUDED_BY_PRODUCT_POLICY` (owner-reviewed rationale). No default inference that all 462 observed leaves require a Web journey, and no blanket `NOT_APPLICABLE` disposition. The reported 385 missing leaf-to-journey links require classification, not deletion.

**P0-D02 — Migration classification.** For each of the reported 349 unresolved and 123 mixed migration blocks, split mixed content into minimal normative assertions, source-only implementation observations, and historical/execution notes. Route normative assertions to *exactly one* owning phase, preserve source provenance/compatibility, and quarantine historical material from active acceptance inputs. No heuristic bulk acceptance.

**P0-D03 — Executable requirements.** A functional operation requirement is admitted only with actor/context, objective, exact inputs/units, valid/invalid cases, preconditions, state/effects, external side effects, recovery, permissions, observables, quality bounds or an explicit `BOUND_TO_PROFILE`, and testable acceptance. Do not invent numeric model accuracy, language coverage, latency, codec, or privacy limits; unresolved commitments are classified as out of admitted profile until independently determined. Every selected provider/profile can be `DEFINED`, `IMPLEMENTED`, `LICENSE_ADMITTED`, `QUALIFIED` and `AVAILABLE` independently.

**P0-D04 — Core supported semantic scope.** Keep source preservation, stable artifact/version references, captions and transcript revision, operator-safe jobs, consent/rights, time fidelity, preview/review, interface compatibility, quality/uncertainty, and safe failures in the authored product. Defer *runtime availability* for models, native transcoding, codecs, special animation/physics, live multi-party and output types without qualification; do not delete their explicit Product Truth requirements if they remain accepted roadmap scope.

**P0 done when:** every applicable capability and normative assertion has an owner-approved, proof-addressable disposition; accepted ProductDefinition has required semantic relationships, not only schema shape; independent P0-010 reviews actual coverage. Current generated `product-definition.candidate.json` is structurally valid but its 19 documented semantic field mappings are *not* accepted.

## 3. PDP-1: canonical behavior and state decisions

**P1-D01 — Typed identity and immutable versions.** All mutable Media entities have tenant-scoped opaque stable identity plus a separately monotonic or content-derived immutable revision where required. `ArtifactId` identifies a logical artifact; `ArtifactVersionId` identifies an exact immutable byte/version result; `ProjectRevisionId`, `CaptionVersionId`, `JobId`, `AttemptId`, and `DeliveryId` are different identities. A checksum does not replace a version, rights, or ownership identity. A derived artifact records exact source-version lineage, transformation, producer/policy/model versions and media-clock mapping.

**P1-D02 — Job, attempt, dispatch and delivery are separate machines.** `(machineId, stateId)` is the state identity. Job-queue acceptance, attempt claim, durable dispatch intent, effect observation, provider finality, delivery acknowledgment and UI observation are not interchangeable. Keep append-only attempts, monotonic fencing, idempotency scope, durable outbox and reconciliation rules as required product semantics. They remain implementation requirements until storage/provider tests demonstrate them.

**P1-D03 — Resolve legacy status conflicts:** `ACCEPTED` = ingress/request accepted but not necessarily queued; `QUEUED` = durable eligible scheduled job. They are NOT synonyms. `RETRY_PENDING` = a job awaits a newly authorized next attempt; `RETRYING` = legacy UI/transport label only, never a canonical job state or an indication that a new attempt ran. `CANCELLING` = legacy observer label only; canonical job state remains whatever its authoritative machine reports and cancellation request/confirmation lives on the attempt/effect dimension. Preserve raw legacy spellings in compatibility projections with explicitly lossy mapping; never normalize unknown/reconciling to `RUNNING` as semantic truth.

**P1-D04 — Finality.** `COMPLETED` needs a persisted valid required-output set, integrity/provenance/rights checks appropriate to its exact contract, fencing validity, and authoritative result commitment; it does not imply delivery acknowledgment. `PARTIALLY_SUCCEEDED` is terminal only where a contract declares independent sub-effects, names accepted partial output, and has a closed disposition for *every* declared sub-effect. Any consequential unknown effect stays `OUTCOME_UNKNOWN`/`RECONCILING`. `FAILED` is final only after no admitted continuation remains. `CANCELLED` requires cancellation finality (or authoritative proof no dispatch occurred) and no unfenced effect able to mutate the job; a cancel request, timeout, UI detach or provider hint is never sufficient. A delivered copy cannot be presumed revoked by local cancellation.

**P1-D05 — Recovery.** Lost acknowledgment, socket close, lease expiry and timeout are uncertainty, not failure or replay permission. Resolve by stable command/attempt identifier and provider/destination reconciliation. Retry requires proven idempotency *or* a new authorized attempt with explicit duplicate-effect assessment, bounds, budget, rights and fencing. User detaching from a job does not cancel it.

**P1-D06 — Consent, rights and erasure.** Consent/rightholder authorization has a specific scope, effective version, purpose, actor and revocation time; revocation blocks future admitted work, triggers bounded downstream propagation/reconciliation, and preserves evidence of irreversible external deliveries or statutory holds. `ERASURE_CONFIRMED` must include provider/object-store obligations and holds resolution; hiding a view is not deletion. C2PA signature/provenance is not permission, ownership or truth.

**P1-D07 — Time and scientific results.** Canonical media time is rational plus clock/timebase and origin; all sample/frame/subtitle, variable-frame-rate, spatial and source-to-output maps require explicit units and conversion/error handling. Preserve unmeasured or unqualified quality as `UNKNOWN`/`NOT_QUALIFIED`, not fabricated confidence or a metric universalized across content domains.

**P1-D08 — Operation taxonomy.** The observed nine logical operation families are *organizational*, not a license to equate every UI action and RPC with a business operation. Every source identity must have a typed disposition: `DOMAIN_COMMAND`, `DOMAIN_QUERY`, `CLIENT_ONLY`, `NAVIGATION_OR_PRESENTATION`, `TRANSPORT_ONLY`, `PROVIDER_ADMIN`, `EVENT_NOTIFICATION`, `INTERNAL_ONLY`, or `NOT_ADMITTED`. Real domain commands additionally bind canonical operation, authorization, input/output/error/finality/version/idempotency/cancellation, and concrete transport/consumer. Disambiguate `request-transcription` as a *submission command* whose response creates/returns a Job; recognition itself is an asynchronous execution, not implicit synchronous success. Caption save/compare need canonical version-write/read semantics, not a blind mapping to STT. Legacy JS tokens `for`, `if`, `clearTimeout` in SDK inventory are parser false positives and must be removed by a parser improvement with a regression, never silently counted as supported API methods. Model loading and provider management are classified to the AI Inference boundary until a distinct Media product capability is approved. Lifecycle notifications are observations, not producer or terminality proof.

**P1 done when:** the reported 50 parity findings are individually resolved *without changing the denominator by guesswork*; 146 UI actions, 27 HTTP operations, 43 gRPC methods, 11 fixture CLI commands, observed SDK/Agent/event surfaces all have an exact classification and source identity; state/operation invariants and negative cases pass; no silent equivalence, permission inference, or runtime activation is claimed.

## 4. PDP-2: approved design and interface policy

**P2-D01 — Shared primitives and Media semantics.** Use `@ghatana/tokens`, theme, design-system/headless and design-system-tooling. Media owns semantic aliases, component intent, view templates, combinations, state grammar and context; Shared owns token values, focus/keyboard primitives and generic reusable behavior. Product UI must not promote Explorer fixture CSS or local semantic color palettes into design authority.

**P2-D02 — Component reuse rule.** The 157-path audit now records 111 archival / 25 fix / 21 reuse / 0 unresolved *inventory dispositions*. This is a useful completed classification, not evidence that 21 independently reusable contracts or all 47 screens are accepted. For every `REUSE`/applicable `FIX`, identify public export, typed props/state/ports, anatomy, accessibility, token lineage, responsive behavior, localization, interaction and regression. Source-path reuse alone is insufficient.

**P2-D03 — Root-cause conformance.** Continue scanning Media UI, AI Voice UI and composed screens; separate Explorer chrome. Deduplicate observations by missing Shared binding, missing template/component or alias authority, invalid interactive grammar, accessibility violation, or a legitimate documented exception. The latest reported zero unexplained product-source observations does not resolve the seven explicit authority/review gates; close those with real decisions and artifact/proof checks, not by changing checker predicates.

**P2-D04 — Interface families.** GUI, HTTP, gRPC, CLI, SDK, Agent Tools and events have independent interface contracts, compatibility and trust/error semantics; a machine-only feature is an experience to explore but does not need a fabricated GUI. Use a neutral affordance inspector for APIs/CLIs with exact request, output, error, auth and lifecycle examples.

**P2 done when:** schema-valid, directly mapped ExperienceLanguage; all required component/layout/token/template chains resolve via public Shared exports; independent visual and assistive-technology review for accepted representative/critical views plus deterministic cross-viewport, zoom, forced-colors, keyboard and localization coverage; no unexplained applicable authority finding. Seven currently outstanding authority/review gates remain open until actually evaluated.

## 5. PDP-3: approved contract-completeness and Explorer policy

**P3-D01 — Complete contracts, not a product shipping gate.** For each of the 47 indexed screens and 30 journeys, accept exact applicable view, channel, state, action, decision/authority, side effect, failure/recovery, component/template, fixture, requirements and test oracle bindings. Do not treat all 47 as production routes; candidate screen links must say `PROPOSAL_PREVIEW` and show unavailable implementations honestly. A source-of-truth phase may close with qualified contract fixtures even while production implementation remains unscheduled, provided every required experience behavior is specified and falsifiable.

**P3-D02 — Shared presentation/port equality.** Product, Tools review and eventual production contexts use the same `MediaProductRenderer` export for compatible GUI cases. Host adapters may vary only data loading, consent/auth, transport, navigation and effects. Test identical props/state/action ports and DOM/accessible behavior for the same fixture and enforce that `createExplorer(...).render` actually invokes/provides the Media renderer rather than only a synthetic projection. For machine channels, use public contracts and Tools inspectors without a DOM requirement.

**P3-D03 — Acceptance by state matrix.** For each applicable critical journey prove happy path and negative: empty/loading/partial/stale, policy-denied, consent-revoked, authority-expired, offline/conflict, unknown-finality/reconcile, duplicate/idempotency, cancel-requested-not-cancelled, retry-disallowed, corrupted media, unsupported format, long job/backpressure, output quality unknown and event delay/replay. Browser audit counts and clean consoles are smoke coverage, not semantic or independent screen-reader signoff.

**P3 done when:** ExperienceDefinition fields are directly mapped and validate under the public Tools package, all selected critical fixtures/cases execute deterministically and fail closed for stale/unauthorized inputs, actual renderer parity is proven for supported families, and unsupported machine/runtime implementations remain explicitly staged.

## 6. Lifecycle decision and closure-state hygiene

**L-D01 — Evidence eligibility.** Preserve four canonical top-level PDP phase identities, but generate requirement-scoped obligations/proof-case memberships and owner-accepted observers/oracles, not four blanket statements as complete proof. Bound proof to normative source fingerprints; use Lifecycle's public provider/admission contracts, not Media-authored fake receipts.

**L-D02 — Transition out of pending is possible.** The existing `scripts/check-media-lifecycle-closure-inputs.mjs` correctly checks current blocked inputs but hardcodes `PENDING`/`BLOCKED` and an empty provider list as eternal success criteria. Refactor to accept current `PENDING` inputs *or* an independently justified input-ready state: zero outstanding decision blockers; admitted nonempty provider bindings and granular case/observer/oracle assignments; currentness and receipt decisions remain exclusively Lifecycle-owned. Fail on premature readiness. Do not relabel `BLOCKED` to make a test green.

**L-D03 — RC1 and provider registry.** Resolve the post-split registry paths through the Lifecycle package's exported manifest/registry identity and versioned public artifact. Reject unresolved sibling-source references, mismatched generated package/Gradle coordinate or `UNLICENSED` evidence-contract distribution. `UNLICENSED` is not a legal finding that the package may be distributed; perform SPDX/SBOM and dependency review before qualifying a release artifact.

**L-D04 — Scope currentness.** A change to one caption timing rule invalidates affected contract/view/test/proof relationships only, not unrelated media phases. Frozen source identity, selected dependency digests and proof method must be checked on every admission; stale/missing/ambiguous/fabricated proof must fail. Local source checks can run without GPU/prod host, but live provider performance can be required where a *specific* product claim depends on it.
