# Ghatana Media product constitution

**Status:** proposed for PDP-0 review; not accepted as full Product Truth
**Canonical structured authority:** [`pdp-0-product-truth/constitution.yaml`](./pdp-0-product-truth/constitution.yaml)

The canonical sequence is PDP-0 Product Truth, PDP-1 Canonical Domain & Data
Model, PDP-2 Design Language & Interface System, and PDP-3 Complete Product
Experience. The Explorer is a projection outside those phases. The surface
inventory is maintained in [`surface-registry.yaml`](./surface-registry.yaml).
The master migration plan is an execution reference, not a semantic authority;
unextracted plan proposals remain open and do not become accepted requirements.

The structured register is the single source for Media's 32 proposed
constitutional requirements, their stable IDs, accountable owner roles,
acceptance owner roles, and review state. This page is a readable pointer to
that authority; it does not define a second editable set of requirements.

Every requirement remains pending human review. Privacy/rights, fidelity, and
publication approval roles are identified in
[`actors-responsibilities.yaml`](./pdp-0-product-truth/actors-responsibilities.yaml),
but human appointments and approvals are not yet recorded. The accepted
[`P0-001 boundary slice`](./pdp-0-product-truth/PRODUCT-TRUTH.md) establishes
the migration boundary and ownership decisions only; it does not accept these
constitutional requirements or claim implementation, qualification, license
admission, runtime availability, or phase acceptance.
