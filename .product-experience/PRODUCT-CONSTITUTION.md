# Ghatana Media product constitution

**Status:** proposed for Phase 0 review; not accepted as full Product Truth  
**Canonical structured authority:** [`phase-0-product-truth/constitution.yaml`](./phase-0-product-truth/constitution.yaml)

The structured register is the single source for Media's 32 proposed
constitutional requirements, their stable IDs, accountable owner roles,
acceptance owner roles, and review state. This page is a readable pointer to
that authority; it does not define a second editable set of requirements.

Every requirement remains pending human review. Privacy/rights, fidelity, and
publication approval roles are identified in
[`actors-responsibilities.yaml`](./phase-0-product-truth/actors-responsibilities.yaml),
but human appointments and approvals are not yet recorded. The accepted
[`P0-001 boundary slice`](./phase-0-product-truth/PRODUCT-TRUTH.md) establishes
the migration boundary and ownership decisions only; it does not accept these
constitutional requirements or claim implementation, qualification, license
admission, runtime availability, or phase acceptance.
