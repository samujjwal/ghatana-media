Quality optimization

DEV_ONLY · owner scoped

A bounded search over real FFmpeg encodes of a video you own. Every attempt is decoded and measured (luma SSIM and PSNR) against the decoded source and listed, including the ones that failed. The best attempt that meets your target becomes a new immutable version; if none does, nothing is chosen. Undo restores the previous version exactly. Not a perceptual, rights, approval or production claim.
