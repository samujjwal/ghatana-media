A synthetic WebAudio tone (no microphone is opened) is cut into 20 ms frames, each with a sequence number, an exact presentation time in samples and a CRC-32, and streamed to the local host. The host orders and de-duplicates frames, tells this page exactly how much it holds, and stores the stream as a WAV plus a per-frame time index only when you end the stream and consent is still admitted. If consent is revoked, the lease expires or you abort, the received audio is destroyed and nothing is stored. No rights clearance, quality, approval or production claim.
