Stems, foley and master

DEV_ONLY · owner scoped

Stems are original audio that Media synthesizes itself (tones, chords, drum hits, noise foley) or uploaded WAVs that carry a processing rights statement from their uploader. The mix is mastered to a loudness target under a true-peak ceiling and stored as a WAV, a lossless FLAC and a JSON report. Every number is measured on the output samples. No rights clearance beyond those statements, no quality, approval or production claim.
