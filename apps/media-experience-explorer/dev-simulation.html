DEV_ONLY · owner scoped

Author a unit-checked mechanics scenario (constant force, damped spring, projectile with drag). A pass that cannot be trusted is reported as abstained, with reasons and no values. Everything shown is simulated model output, never a measurement. No rights, approval, quality or production claim.
